## Stonemasonry across Cheltenham

Cheltenham sits right on the edge of the Cotswolds, and its building stock shows it. Alongside the famous Regency terraces there are thousands of Cotswold stone properties — Victorian villas in Charlton Kings and Leckhampton, cottages in Prestbury and Swindon Village, and post-war and modern houses faced in reconstituted or natural stone. Behind almost all of them: stone garden and boundary walls, many now a century or more old.

We're based in Winchcombe, a 15-minute drive over Cleeve Hill, and Cheltenham makes up a large share of our workload.

## What we do in Cheltenham

- **Garden and boundary wall repair** — Cheltenham's mature gardens are full of ageing stone walls; we rebuild collapsed and bulging sections, refix copings and repoint in lime. See [stone wall repair](/stone-wall-repair/).
- **Stone restoration** — repointing, stone replacement and repair on period properties across the town, including work in Cheltenham's conservation areas and on listed buildings.
- **Stone cleaning** — lifting grime, algae and staining from stone frontages, porches, steps and garden walls without damaging the surface.
- **Dry stone walling** — new garden features and boundary walls in traditional dry stone, particularly on the Cotswold fringe of town: Prestbury, Southam, Charlton Kings, Leckhampton and up onto Cleeve Hill.
- **New stonework and carving** — stone for extensions and landscaping, plus hand-carved house names and numbers for Cheltenham properties.
- **[House signs and numbers](/house-signs/)** — hand-carved name plaques and door numbers for Cheltenham’s Regency villas and terraces, where a painted or plain-cut sign suits the elevation better than a bought one.

## Working in a Regency town

Cheltenham's centre carries extensive conservation-area protection, and many properties are listed. Even where a building is stucco-fronted, the boundary walls, steps, copings and garden structures are often natural stone — and repairs to them are expected to be done sympathetically: matched stone, lime mortar, correct joint finishes. That's how we work by default, and we're happy to provide the detail conservation officers and managing agents ask for.
