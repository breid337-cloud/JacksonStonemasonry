## Stonemasons for the Cotswolds

Everything people love about the Cotswolds — the golden villages, the field walls threading over the hills, the way whole towns seem to have grown out of the ground — is the work of generations of stonemasons using one remarkable material: oolitic Cotswold limestone. It cuts cleanly when fresh, hardens as it weathers, and ranges in colour from the deep honey of the north Cotswolds to the pale grey-cream of the south.

Keeping that landscape looking the way it does isn't a heritage abstraction; it's practical, local work — a rebuilt gap in a field wall, a repointed cottage frontage, a re-carved finial — done properly, in the right stone, with the right mortar. That's the trade we're in.

## What we do across the Cotswolds

- **[Dry stone walling](/dry-stone-walling/)** — the region's signature craft: new walls, rebuilds and repairs in traditional style.
- **[Stone restoration](/stone-restoration/)** — lime repointing, matched stone replacement and sympathetic repair of period buildings.
- **[Stone wall repair](/stone-wall-repair/)** — collapsed, bulging and vehicle-damaged walls rebuilt with their original stone.
- **[Stone cleaning](/stone-cleaning/)** — careful, tested cleaning that respects the weathered face of soft limestone.
- **[Stonemasonry](/stonemasonry/)** — new stonework for extensions, gardens and new builds, matched to the local vernacular.
- **[Stone carving](/stone-carving/)** — hand-carved house signs, date stones and replacement architectural detail.

## Working in a protected landscape

Most of the Cotswolds falls within the Cotswolds National Landscape (formerly the AONB), and its towns and villages carry extensive conservation-area and listed-building protection. In practice that means work on stone is expected to be done traditionally — matched local stone, lime mortars, honest detail — and that planning and consent questions come up often. We work within these frameworks constantly and can advise early, before designs or applications are fixed.
