## The problem

The cottage dates to around 1800 and is built from traditional Cotswold stone. Over the years its joints had been filled with a bad mix of cement and mortar, much of it smeared across the faces of the stones, and the whole elevation had dulled under green growth and staining.

Up close, the old pointing was a patchwork of hard grey cement over earlier mortar, cracked and coming away from the stone. Behind it, some stones were broken or badly set, some cornerstones had failed, and some turned out to be no more than a thin veneer.

## What we did

Charlie and Elliot cleaned the stonework and carefully removed the old mortar back to the stone. Broken, badly set and veneer stones were taken out and replaced, cornerstones included, and Charlie rebuilt the walling under two of the windows.

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/img/work-cockleford-raked-out.jpg" srcset="/assets/img/work-cockleford-raked-out-640.jpg 640w, /assets/img/work-cockleford-raked-out.jpg 1600w" sizes="(min-width: 60rem) 640px, 92vw" alt="The wall below a ground-floor window opened up during the work, old mortar raked out and failed stones removed, with a pile of rubble at its foot" loading="lazy" width="1600" height="516">
  <figcaption>During: the wall under a window opened up, ready to be rebuilt.</figcaption>
</figure>

They then repointed the whole elevation in lime mortar. The colour mattered: samples were discussed with the homeowners to settle on a soft, warm tone that sits with the natural variation in the Cotswold stone rather than fighting it.

Charlie and Elliot kept the site clean throughout. At the end of each working day the paving was brushed, the rubble collected and the tools stored away, and the homeowners were kept informed of the working arrangements and any change to the schedule.

## The result

A front elevation that looks crisp and defined again, without losing any of the cottage's character. The lime mortar brings out the variation in the stone and gives the whole face a more even finish, and the work was completed to the agreed specification and at the quoted price.
