Jackson's Stonemasonry Ltd is a stonemasonry business based in **Winchcombe, Gloucestershire**, working across Cheltenham, the Cotswolds and the surrounding towns and villages.

We work in the traditional trades of the area: [dry stone walling](/dry-stone-walling/), [stone restoration](/stone-restoration/), [stonemasonry](/stonemasonry/), [stone cleaning](/stone-cleaning/), [stone carving](/stone-carving/) and [stone wall repair](/stone-wall-repair/). Most of our work is in Cotswold limestone — the stone this landscape is built from — using the materials and techniques it was built with: hand-worked stone, lime mortars, and walling methods that predate the cement bag by centuries.

## How we work

- **Look first, quote second.** We visit, or work from your photos, and diagnose what's actually wrong before pricing anything.
- **Written, itemised, free quotations.** You'll know exactly what's included, what's essential and what's optional — with no obligation.
- **Honest advice.** If a wall doesn't need rebuilding, or stonework doesn't need cleaning, we'll say so. Most of our work comes from recommendation, and that's how it stays.
- **Traditional materials.** Lime mortar on traditional buildings, stone matched to your property, details copied from the originals.
- **Fully insured.** Full public liability insurance on every job.

## Where we work

We're based in [Winchcombe](/areas/winchcombe/) and work throughout [Cheltenham](/areas/cheltenham/), [Gloucester](/areas/gloucester/), [Tewkesbury](/areas/tewkesbury/), [Broadway](/areas/broadway/), [Stow-on-the-Wold](/areas/stow-on-the-wold/), [Cirencester](/areas/cirencester/) and the wider [Cotswolds](/areas/cotswolds/).
